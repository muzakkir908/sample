## About the game

WICKLIGHT – The Little Candle is a small 2D pixel-art platform game. The player is a little candle in a dark cave. In 60 seconds, the candle must collect 5 embers, jump over spikes, stay away from 2 moths and reach the exit door. The player has 3 lives. The controls are the left arrow, the right arrow and the spacebar (jump). Every ember is near a danger, so each reward has a small risk.
